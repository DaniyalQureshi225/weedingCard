Dholki Night Highlights

Authentic Pakistani traditions, festive music, and heartwarming hospitality

Dholak, Tappay & Dance

Join us for traditional Pakistani wedding geet, impromptu tappay battles, dholak chamach rhythm, and joyous dance performances by family and friends.

Music & Dance

Vibrant Dholki Attire

Dress in vibrant wedding colors: mustard yellow, rani pink, parrot green, bright orange, embroidered kurtas, ghagras, and colorful dupattas.

Dress Code

Gajras & Glass Bangles

Receive fresh fragrant jasmine gajras and pick your favorite colorful glass bangles from our traditional wedding churi counter.

Traditions

Mithai & Festive Feast

Savor hot crispy jalebis, piping hot Kashmiri chai, street-style samosa chaat, followed by a delicious traditional Pakistani dinner buffet.

Refreshments
